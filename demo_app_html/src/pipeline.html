Sequence to Half-Life

Nine-mer sequences become predicted complexes, complexes become frozen MACE descriptors, and a shallow head maps those descriptors onto measured dissociation half-lives. The structure shown is the real Boltz-2 output for ALLENIHRV, the strongest binder in the set with a prediction on disk.

01 / DATA

Measured peptides

1,023 nine-mers measured on this allele in the Rasmussen set. The target is log(t½ / 1 h); the 16 entries recorded as zero are excluded and counted.

02 / STRUCTURE

Boltz complexes

Each peptide is folded with the shared heavy chain and β2m. The example here scores 0.988 complex pLDDT and contributes 74 peptide heavy atoms.

03 / MODEL

Frozen MACE, shallow head

Invariant scalars are read before the energy readout, mean-pooled per peptide position, concatenated, and regressed. Only the head has gradients.

Coordinates are real; the feature widths are not. The peptide, the contact graph and the platform trace are read from predictions/all75/hla_a_02_01_allenihrv/. The descriptor and head dimensions are placeholders until the checkpoint is inspected. Boltz writes heavy atoms only, so hydrogens enter at preparation, before MACE sees the complex.
